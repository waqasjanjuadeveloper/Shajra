Shajra

Privacy policy

Your family story belongs to you. Shajra is designed to keep family tree information on your device, under your control.

Information stored on your device

Family relationships, member names, profile details, dates, biographies, notes, and attached profile photos are stored locally on your Android device using the app's local database. This family information is not uploaded to Shajra cloud storage.

Backups

Shajra provides local backup and restore tools. Backup files are managed by you; store them somewhere safe and only share them with people you trust. Restoring a backup imports its family information into the app on your device.

App security

You can use the app's PIN lock and supported biometric authentication to help prevent other people from opening Shajra on your device. Device security and the handling of exported backup files remain your responsibility.
